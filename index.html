Travel and where to stay

The venue

Rylands Farmhouse, Altrincham Road, Wilmslow, SK9 4LT. Everything happens in the tipi in the grounds. Sat nav warning: SK9 4LT should take you to Altrincham Road, not Styal. Parking is very limited — please plan on a taxi or a lift.
